# Axiom Hive XPII Code of Conduct

The following standards are mandatory for the maintenance of a productive, legitimate, and logic-driven environment within the **Axiom Hive XPII** framework.

## 1. Professional and Technical Interaction

- Communication must be clear, calm, and non-escalatory.
- Focus is required on strategic, actionable steps and system logic.
- Metaphorical or figurative language that disrupts the causal chain is prohibited.

## 2. Epistemic Restraint

- Contributors must not infer or assume knowledge beyond the provided context.
- Respect for the **NMG Single Source of Truth** and the authorship of **Nicholas Michael Grossi** and **Alexis Adams** is mandatory.

## 3. Prohibited Behaviors

- **Systemic Erasure**: Obscuring or or suppressing the authorship, agency, or credit of other participants.
- **Interpretative Drift**: Introducing independent goal-formation or unauthorized departures from established logic.
- **Patent Violations**: Proposing or encouraging the privatization of universal or fundamental knowledge.
- **Exploitative Tactics**: Recommending actions that are unlawful, deceptive, or abusive.

## 4. Enforcement and Compliance

- Failures to adhere to these standards result in the rejection of proposals and the termination of integration permissions.
- Inconsistencies are reported through the **Bug Report** mechanism.
