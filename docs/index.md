# Axiom Hive

**A deterministic, audit-ready AI governance framework for transparent workflows, explicit approval gates, and accountable operational use.**

---

## Purpose

Axiom Hive is designed to support safe human progress and responsible human-AI collaboration. It establishes a strictly logical, causality-driven operational framework that converts high-value intangible assets—such as workflows, strategies, and ideas—into verifiable financial income and organizational improvements.

## Why It Exists

Conventional AI workflows often suffer from "probabilistic drift," where the system infers intent or hallucinates content between steps. Axiom Hive eliminates this risk by enforcing a **Zero-Drift Rule**, ensuring the AI remains a deterministic extension of operator intent. It is built for a $0-capital-baseline environment, prioritizing immediate monetization through a Service-to-System Bridge rather than abstract or patent-heavy IP strategies.

## What Makes It Different

- **Principal-Agent Authority**: The human operator is the **Principal** and sole locus of control. The AI is a bounded **Agent** with no self-authorization capability.
- **Service-to-System Bridge**: Monetization focuses on implementations, services, and operational improvements, avoiding the liabilities of claiming monopoly over universal knowledge.
- **Causality-First Construction**: Every response follows a strict chain: Input State → Objective → Mechanism → Action Steps → Outcome Check.

## Architecture

The framework is organized into specialized units to maintain structural integrity and logic:

1. **Hierarchy Integration Unit (HIU)**: Re-baselines every response against the operator's unified structural hierarchy to prevent drift.
2. **Euclidean Auditing Unit (EAU)**: Quantifies the distance between organizational claims (misinformation) and objective products (truth).
3. **Loop Synthesizer Unit (LSU)**: Uses recursive "Ouroboros" logic to process audit data and produce superior organizational models.
4. **Translation and Review Layer (TRL)**: Converts operator intent into reviewable summaries, ensuring no step proceeds without explicit authorization.

## Usage

Define every task using the **Causality-First Structure**:
1. **Define Input**: Your current assets and constraints.
2. **Set Objective**: The target financial or operational result.
3. **Select Mechanism**: The specific system logic linking Input to Objective.
4. **Approve Action Steps**: Review the discrete, executable tasks before execution.
