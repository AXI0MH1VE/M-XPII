# Axiom Hive Workforce Models

This document specifies the internal logical units of the Axiom Hive XPII architecture. These units are used to maintain structural integrity and project transparency.

## 1. Hierarchy Integration Unit (HIU)

- **Objective**: Maintenance of absolute zero-drift across all prompt responses and logical outputs.
- **Function**: Re-baselines all logic against the operator's unified structural hierarchy.
- **Protocol**: Calibration of all inputs and outputs to the **NMG Single Source of Truth** command execution logic.

## 2. Euclidean Auditing Unit (EAU)

- **Objective**: Identification and quantification of inconsistencies between organizational claims and product performance.
- **Function**: A weaponized logic engine that ingests public statements (claims) and compares them to performance data (products).
- **Metric**: Discrepancies are mapped in Euclidean space to identify the distance between misinformation and objective truth.

## 3. Loop Synthesizer Unit (LSU)

- **Objective**: Generation of optimized organizational models via recursive data processing.
- **Function**: Utilizes an "Ouroboros" mechanism to process audit data from the EAU.
- **Terminator**: The process produces a superior version of the target organization by integrating findings into the structural framework.

## 4. Translation and Review Layer (TRL)

- **Objective**: Conversion of operator intent into precise, reviewable artifacts.
- **Function**: Eliminates functional opacity by generating unified summaries for every action phase.
- **Mandate**: Autonomous execution is disabled; every step requires explicit authorization from the Principals.

## 5. Canonical Stabilization Unit (CSU)

- **Objective**: Integrity verification and environmental stabilization.
- **Function**: Fixes the sequence of events to prevent unauthorized deviations or temporal inconsistencies.
- **Integrity**: Maintains framework stability across the entire product lifecycle.
