# Example: E-commerce AOV Optimization (Axiom Hive)

This example demonstrates the logic-first optimization of a large-scale e-commerce operation using $0 liquid capital.

## Input State

- **Current assets**: 50,000 monthly transactions, stagnant Average Order Value (AOV), and high customer-churn data.
- **Tools**: Access to transaction logs and automated customer-feedback streams.
- **Constraints**: $0 capital for new analytics software.

## Objective

Identify the exact friction in the logic that is preventing up-selling at checkout.

## Mechanism: Euclidean Auditing Unit (EAU)

The EAU is used to map the distance between the organization's "Claimed" value-proposition ("We offer relevant recommendations") and the "Actual" product performance ("Recommendations are ignored 98% of the time").

### Logic Steps:
1.  **Ingest Product Data**: Link checkout items to recommendation logs.
2.  **Audit the Inconsistency**: Quantify the Euclidean distance between customer interest (search history) and system output (upsell offer).
3.  **Identify Friction**: The EAU finds that recommendation logic relies on high-inventory items rather than high-relevance items, creating "Service-to-System" drift.

## Implementation: Service-to-System Bridge

Instead of a patent-heavy software replacement, the operator offers a **Performance Audit** service to the e-commerce decision-maker.

### Action Steps:
1.  **Draft findings**: A one-page Strategic Efficiency Roadmap showing the recommendation logic gap.
2.  **Performance Offer**: "Reduce churn by 5% through relevance-logic calibration—no upfront cost, 20% of realized upside."
3.  **Verification**: The operator runs a manual, small-scale test of the high-relevance recommendations on a subset of users to prove the logic before scale.

## Outcome Check

- **Success**: Positive response requesting a 15-minute walkthrough of the EAU audit findings.
- **Validation**: Realized AOV increase of 4% within the tested segment.
- **Result**: Immediate financial income generated from logic-based consulting without upfront capital.
